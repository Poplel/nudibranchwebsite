Contact

Nudibranch is made by one person. Replies are cheerful but not instant.

Email: me@poplel.xyz — for anything at all.

Pick the right door

  • Something's broken

    Open an issue on GitHub. Include what you did, what happened, and the relevant lines from the application log — Activity → Log on the server.

  • An idea or a feature request

    Also GitHub issues. Say what you're trying to accomplish, not only the feature you have in mind — it often points somewhere better.

  • A question about the app

    Try the FAQ first. If it doesn't cover it, email is fine.

  • Privacy or data questions

    The privacy policy answers most of them. Anything it doesn't, email — it's the same address listed there.

Security disclosures

If you've found a vulnerability in the server, the app or the push relay, please email me@poplel.xyz rather than opening a public issue. A description of the problem and how to reproduce it is plenty. You'll get an acknowledgement, and credit in the fix if you'd like it.

What this isn't

Nudibranch servers are run by the people who own them. If you use someone else's server and something about your account is wrong — a password, a permission, a missing album — the person who runs it is the one who can fix it. There's no account with us to reset.
