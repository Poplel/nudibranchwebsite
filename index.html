Nudibranch

A self-hosted music library manager & player, with an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Nudibranch is a remote. Your music, playlists and listening history live on a server you run. The app talks to that server and nothing else.

What it does

  • Browse and play your library — offline-first, with downloads for the road.
  • Review and approve library changes: imports, downloads, metadata tools.
  • Podcasts, playlists, and a wishlist for things you don't own yet.
  • Background audio, CarPlay, lock-screen transport, Siri and Shortcuts.

Getting it

The server is open source and available on GitHub. The iOS app is on its way to the App Store.

Privacy

Nudibranch collects nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking and no account with us — read the privacy policy for the details.
