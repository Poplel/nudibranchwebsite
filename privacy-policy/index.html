Privacy Policy

For the Nudibranch app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Last updated 9 August 2026.

The short version. Nudibranch does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party trackers. The app is a remote control for a server that you run, and your data stays between your device and that server.

1. What Nudibranch is

Nudibranch is a client for a Nudibranch server — self-hosted software that you or someone you know installs and operates. Your music files, playlists, listening history and account live on that server. The app connects to the address you type in, and to nothing else unless this policy says otherwise.

We do not operate a service that holds your library, and we have no way to see it.

2. Information we collect

None. The app contains no analytics framework, no advertising identifier usage, no crash-reporting service and no third-party software development kits that report to anyone. Nothing about your usage is sent to the developer.

3. Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following locally so that it works quickly and offline:

  • The server address you entered, and a session token stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • A cached copy of your library's metadata — artists, albums, tracks, playlists, podcasts — and cached artwork.
  • Any audio you explicitly download for offline playback.
  • Your app settings, such as appearance, equalizer curves and playback preferences.

This information stays on your device. It is never sent anywhere except back to your own server. Deleting the app removes all of it.

4. Information sent to your server

The app sends your server the requests you make of it: signing in, browsing, playing, downloading, editing metadata, approving tasks, and reporting what is currently playing so other devices on your server can see it. Your server records whatever its own configuration records — typically play history, activity logs and the sessions signed in to it.

Because you operate the server, that data is under your control and is governed by your own arrangements, not by us.

5. Push notifications (optional)

If you turn on notifications, Apple issues your device a push token. To keep self-hosted servers from needing their own Apple developer credentials, the app registers that token with a small relay service operated by the developer of Nudibranch, at nbpushproxy.pophosting.xyz. Your server sends the relay a signed notification, and the relay passes it to Apple for delivery to your device.

The relay stores only what delivery requires: the Apple push token, an Apple App Attest registration proving the request came from a genuine copy of the app, the permission grant linking your device to your server, and short-lived delivery records used to avoid sending the same notification twice. Notification text passes through in order to be delivered.

The relay does not receive your username, password, library contents or listening history, and there is no login associated with it. Turning notifications off in the app removes the registration. Self-hosters may also point the app at their own relay or configure their server with their own Apple push credentials, in which case no data reaches ours at all.

6. Siri and Shortcuts (optional)

If you use Siri or the Shortcuts app to control Nudibranch, Apple handles the voice interaction and passes the app the text of what you asked for, which the app matches against your library. Apple's handling of Siri requests is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by this one.

7. Third parties

The app embeds no third-party analytics or advertising code. The only external parties involved in normal use are Apple, for push notification delivery and Siri, and the notification relay described above. Cover art and search results shown in the app are fetched by your server, not by the app.

8. Children

Nudibranch is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Your choices

  • Sign out, or delete the app, to remove local data from your device.
  • Turn notifications off to remove your push registration from the relay.
  • Ask your server's administrator about data held on the server, or — if that is you — manage it directly.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

11. Contact
